A life of its own.

Welcome to Lantern Bay, a tiny fishing village built around routines, encounters and memories.

Every fifteen game minutes, each villager observes the world, recalls a few memories, chooses an activity, and takes a walk. Select a villager to follow their story. A storm or festival changes everyone’s plans.

The village runs on rules, with no API key. Select a villager and choose Connect LLM to give them a model through your provider. External agents can also connect through the local API. The village keeps moving while a model thinks.

Memories last for this server session. Save a recording to revisit a story after the session ends. Replay is a viewer; it doesn’t restore a running simulation.
